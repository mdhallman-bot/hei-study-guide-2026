Key concepts

Humans and the environment affect each other in both directions.

AdaptationModification
MeaningPeople change their behavior to fit the environment.People change the environment itself.
ExamplesWearing coats in winter, irrigation, building sheltersBuilding dams, roads and cities; clearing land for farms
DirectionEnvironment affects humansHumans affect the environment

Quick check: if the person adjusts, it is adaptation. If the landscape changes, it is modification.

Humans changing the environment

  • Modification: cities, dams, roads, agriculture
  • Positive effects: better living conditions, more food
  • Negative effects: habitat loss, pollution, biodiversity loss

The environment affecting humans

  • Adaptation: appropriate clothing, irrigation, shelters
  • People rely on the environment for water, food and energy

Sustainability

  • Conserve forests, water and energy
  • Sustainable practices: crop rotation, recycling, planting trees

Desertification

  • Causes: overgrazing, deforestation, poor farming, climate change
  • Effects: soil degradation, less food, forced migration
  • Solutions: reforestation, sustainable farming, water management

Climate change and biodiversity

  • Pollution and deforestation speed up climate change
  • Climate change and habitat loss threaten biodiversity
  • Conservation and sustainable practices protect ecosystems

Cause and effect chain

  • Urbanization or farming leads to deforestation
  • Deforestation leads to habitat loss and more carbon in the air
  • Both lead to lower biodiversity and a changing climate
